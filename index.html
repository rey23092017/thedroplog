Pre-order Launched 22 Jul 2026 Foldable — ultra flagship

Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra

Samsung's Unpacked event in London just introduced a genuinely new tier — the Z Fold8 Ultra is the first-ever "Ultra" branded foldable, sitting above a redesigned, wider standard Z Fold8 built more for media viewing. At 215g and 0.41cm folded thin, it's the lightest Fold Samsung has built, though the same chip shortage squeezing the rest of this list pushed pricing up by roughly ₹43,000 over last year's equivalent. Pre-orders are open now in India, shipping from 8 August.
